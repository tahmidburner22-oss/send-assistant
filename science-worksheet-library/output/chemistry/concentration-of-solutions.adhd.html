WHAT IS A SOLUTION?
THE FORMULA YOU NEED
concentration (g/dm³) = mass of solute (g)volume of solution (dm³)
Conversion Reminder
1000 cm³ = 1 dm³
WORKED EXAMPLE (FOLLOW THIS!)
12 g of sodium chloride is dissolved in 0.75 dm³ of water.
Find the concentration.
Step 1: Write the formula:
concentration = mass ÷ volume
Step 2: Put in the numbers:
= 12 g ÷ 0.75 dm³
Step 3: Answer = 16 g/dm³

FIND THE MASS
A solution has a concentration of 24 g/dm³. The volume is 0.40 dm³.
Work out the mass of solute.
Rearrange: mass = concentration × volume
Put in the numbers: 24 × 0.40
Calculate and write the unit (g)
Answer:g
