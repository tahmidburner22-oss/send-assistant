FORCES – FORCES & MOTION
KEY VOCABULARY
Resultant force: the single force that has the same effect as all forces.
Weight: the force of gravity on a mass; W = m × g.
Acceleration: the rate of change of velocity, in m/s².
Terminal velocity: steady speed when drag balances weight.
COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS
✗ A moving object always has a force pushing it.✓ At constant speed, forces are balanced.
✗ Mass and weight are the same thing.✓ Mass is in kg; weight is a force in newtons.
✗ Heavier objects always fall faster.✓ Without air, all objects fall at the same rate.
