WAVES – PROPERTIES OF WAVES
KEY VOCABULARY
Wavelength (λ): distance from one wave crest to the next, in metres.
Frequency (f): number of waves per second, measured in hertz (Hz).
Amplitude: maximum displacement from the rest position.
Transverse: vibrations are at 90° to the direction of travel.
COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS
✗ Waves transfer matter from place to place.✓ Waves transfer energy, not matter.
✗ Amplitude is the same as wavelength.✓ Amplitude is height; wavelength is length.
✗ Sound is a transverse wave.✓ Sound is a longitudinal wave.
PARTS OF A TRANSVERSE WAVE
Wave speed: v = f × λ (speed = frequency × wavelength).
