BIOENERGETICS – PHOTOSYNTHESIS
KEY VOCABULARY
Photosynthesis: plants make glucose using light energy.
Chlorophyll: green pigment that absorbs light energy.
Endothermic: a reaction that takes in energy.
Limiting factor: the factor that stops the rate increasing further.
COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS
✗ Plants only respire at night.✓ Plants respire all the time, day and night.
✗ Plants get food from the soil.✓ Plants make glucose in their leaves.
✗ Photosynthesis releases energy.✓ It is endothermic — it absorbs light energy.
INPUTS & OUTPUTS
carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (light/chlorophyll).
