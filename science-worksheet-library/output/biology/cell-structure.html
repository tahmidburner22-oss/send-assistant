CELL BIOLOGY – ANIMAL CELL STRUCTURE
KEY VOCABULARY
Nucleus: controls the cell and contains the genetic material (DNA).
Cytoplasm: jelly-like fluid where most reactions happen.
Mitochondria: site of aerobic respiration; releases energy.
Ribosomes: where protein synthesis takes place.
COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS
✗ Animal cells have a cell wall.✓ Only plant, fungal and bacterial cells have walls.
✗ The nucleus makes energy.✓ Mitochondria release energy in respiration.
✗ All cells are the same size and shape.✓ Cells are specialised for their function.
