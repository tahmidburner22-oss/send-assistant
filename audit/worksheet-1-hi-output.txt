By the end of this lesson, students will be able to explain the processes of photosynthesis and respiration, and their interdependence in bioenergetics.

Photosynthesis:
The process by which green plants and some other organisms use sunlight to synthesise foods with the help of chlorophyll pigment.nRespiration — The process by which organisms combine oxygen with food molecules, diverting the energy in these substances into life-sustaining activities and releasing carbon dioxide and water.nChlorophyll — The green pigment found in chloroplasts that absorbs light energy from the sun.nATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) — The molecule that provides energy for cellular processes.nMitochondria — The organelles found in large numbers in most cells, in which the biochemical processes of respiration and energy production occur.
Watch out for these common errors:
MISTAKE 1: CONFUSING PHOTOSYNTHESIS AND RESPIRATION. → PHOTOSYNTHESIS USES LIGHT ENERGY TO MAKE GLUCOSE AND OXYGEN FROM CARBON DIOXIDE AND WATER. RESPIRATION BREAKS DOWN GLUCOSE USING OXYGEN TO RELEASE ENERGY, PRODUCING CARBON DIOXIDE AND WATER.
MISTAKE 2: THINKING RESPIRATION ONLY HAPPENS IN ANIMALS. → RESPIRATION OCCURS IN ALL LIVING ORGANISMS, INCLUDING PLANTS, FUNGI, AND BACTERIA, TO RELEASE ENERGY FROM GLUCOSE.
MISTAKE 3: NOT UNDERSTANDING THE ROLE OF ATP. → ATP IS THE IMMEDIATE ENERGY CURRENCY OF THE CELL. GLUCOSE IS A STORAGE MOLECULE; ITS ENERGY MUST BE CONVERTED INTO ATP FOR CELLS TO USE IT.

— Study this example carefully.
1
nCalculate the energy released from 10g of glucose during aerobic respiration.
nGiven: Energy released from 1g of glucose = 15 kJ/g
nStep 1: Identify the knowns. Mass of glucose = 10g. Energy per gram = 15 kJ/g.nStep 2: Identify the unknown. Total energy released.nStep 3: Apply the formula: Total Energy = Mass × Energy per gram.nStep 4: Substitute values: Total Energy = 10g × 15 kJ/g.nStep 5: Calculate the answer: Total Energy = 150 kJ. ✓ Key point: Ensure units are consistent before calculation and include them in the final answer.
