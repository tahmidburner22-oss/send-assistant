Anxiety / Mental Health
Social, Emotional & Mental Health

HOW IT PRESENTS

Pupils may appear quiet and compliant but find it hard to begin, re-read the same question repeatedly, under-perform when asked to show working, or freeze on challenge questions. Physical signs include trembling, fidgeting, or avoidance of eye contact with the task.

BARRIERS ON A STANDARD WORKSHEET

High-stakes language ('must', 'quickly', 'only N minutes'), mandatory challenge questions, unpredictable formatting, and reflection prompts that require exposing uncertainty all amplify threat response and shut down learning.

WHAT THE GENERATOR CHANGES

Section A is renamed 'Warm-Up — no pressure!', the challenge is clearly 'Optional Bonus', each section opens with a positive priming line, obligation words are swapped for invitation words, and an emoji check-in bookends the worksheet. No time pressure language anywhere.

WHAT WILL CHANGE IN YOUR WORKSHEET

Section A renamed 'Warm-Up — no pressure!'; challenge is 'OPTIONAL BONUS'; warm encouraging tone throughout; emoji check-in at start and end; no threatening language.

Section A renamed 'Warm-Up — no pressure!'

Why: Anxiety activates the threat response which shuts down the prefrontal cortex needed for learning; reducing perceived threat is the first priority

Challenge clearly labelled 'OPTIONAL BONUS — only if you want to!'

Why: Mandatory challenge questions create performance anxiety; making them optional removes the fear of failure

Positive statement at the start of each section ('You already know this — let's practise!')

Why: Positive priming reduces anticipatory anxiety and activates approach motivation rather than avoidance motivation

'How are you feeling?' emoji check-in at start and end

Why: Emotional regulation check-ins are a core component of the Zones of Regulation framework recommended for anxiety in the SEND Code of Practice

'Must', 'should', 'need to' replaced with 'try to', 'have a go at'

Why: Obligation language triggers anxiety responses; invitational language achieves the same goal without the threat

Teacher section included: The generated content will contain a private teacher-only section explaining every adaptation made and the evidence-based rationale for why it helps students with Anxiety / Mental Health.
